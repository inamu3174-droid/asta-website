Our Story

Some things are better brought closer.

Velora began with a simple idea to bring a little piece of Kashmir closer to you.

From the warmth of its homes to the things gathered from its land, we choose the flavours that feel familiar, honest and worth sharing.

Honey. Saffron. Kahwa. Dry fruits.

We’re not here to make Kashmir louder.
Just a little closer.

From here, to you.
