Our Story

Some things are better brought closer.

ASTA began with a simple idea to bring a little piece of Kashmir closer to you.

From the warmth of its homes to the things gathered from its land, we choose the flavours that feel familiar, honest and worth sharing.

Honey. Saffron. Kahwa. Dry fruits.

Different traditions, one place we call home.

We’re not here to make Kashmir louder.
Just a little closer.

From here, to you.

How to Brew

01

Boil

Bring 120 ml of water to a rolling boil in a pan.

02

Infuse

Add 1 teaspoon of Kahwa powder and simmer on low heat for 3 to 4 minutes.

03

Garnish

Natural Characteristic

This untreated honey naturally thickens and becomes opaque in winters, and thins out in summers. This natural variation is characteristic of honey.

Biological Source — Gently gathered by honeybees from the untouched white blossoms of native acacia trees. Contains no refined additives; its clean sweetness comes entirely from pure, natural floral sugars.

Ingredients — Raw White Honey.

Care — Store in a cool, dry place. Do not refrigerate. If thickened, place the jar in warm water to liquefy.
