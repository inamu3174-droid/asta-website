The Ritual

How to Brew

01

Boil

Bring 120ml of water to a rolling boil in a pan.

02

Infuse

Add 1 teaspoon of Kahwa powder and simmer on low heat for 3 to 4 minutes.

03

Garnish

Strain into a cup, add the crushed almond slices to taste, and enjoy.
